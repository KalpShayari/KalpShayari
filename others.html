Aankhon Ka Aaina

Nazm
Maine tumhe dekha, Tumhari aankhon mein dekha, Tumhari aankhon mein khudko dekha, Khudko dekha, khudki aankhon mein dekha, Khudki aankhon mein maine tumhe dekha.
